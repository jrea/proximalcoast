X Original Content Rewards: dataset notes
November 23, 2026

The CSV contains the 464 payouts used in “The Reality of Content Rewards,” reported publicly by 215 creators across 251 X posts. Each row is one creator and payout cycle, in USD. A single post may show several payouts in its payment history, so the same source link can appear on multiple rows.

Included: exact positive amounts publicly reported by the creator and linked to X Original Content Rewards. Evidence includes labeled payout records, first-person disclosures and supporting public sources. Excluded: duplicates for the same creator and cycle, cumulative totals, predictions, obvious satire, separate bonuses, legacy revenue sharing and amounts we could not confidently attribute to this program.

How to read the data
- Payout cycle is the payment period label, not the posting date. Later posts sometimes show older payments.
- The views column is a snapshot of public views on the payout disclosure, not views or verified impressions for the content that earned the payout. Blank means missing or not attributable, not zero.
- Follower counts are public snapshots, not follower counts at the time of payment. Observation dates are shown when recorded. Blank means missing.
- Source links point to the original public disclosures. Supporting links are included when relevant. Links may change or disappear.

Reproducing the graphic
- Total: $272,067.86. Average: $586.35. Median: $227.845 (shown as $228). Exactly 232 of 464 payouts were below $228.
- 109 under $100; 218 from $100 to under $500; 70 from $500 to under $1,000; 67 at $1,000 or more.
- Sort payout amounts highest to lowest. The top 47 rows account for 51.16% of all dollars in the sample; the top 93 account for 67.71%.
- 54 of 242 disclosure posts with recorded view counts had more than 10,000 public views.

This is a sample of people who chose to share a positive payout publicly. It excludes zero payouts and people who did not disclose. It cannot tell you what a typical X creator earns or what any creator should expect.
